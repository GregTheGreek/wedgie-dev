wedgie.dev

You've got a wedgie.

A pocket computer: a Raspberry Pi Pico, a screen, and a secure chip wedged in between. It runs one app at a time: games, tools, or a wallet signer.

The buttons

Green says yes. Red says no. The joystick and gray buttons do whatever the app wants.

Get started

  1. Plug it into a computer with a USB-C data cable (some cables only charge). The screen lights up. No light? Turn the cable around: swap which end goes in the wedgie. Some only work one way.
  2. A drive called WEDGIE shows up. Open Open wedgie on it, or go to wedgie.dev/connect in Chrome or Edge. Safari and Firefox can't talk to USB.
  3. Click Connect and pick your wedgie.
  4. Update the firmware if it asks. Always. Press green on the wedgie.
  5. Pick an app. The wedgie asks Install? on its screen. Press green.

A new wedgie says No software until you pick an app. That's normal.

Apps

Games: Frog, Grove, Bunker, Blocks, 2048, Blackjack. Tools: Buttons, Keypad. And Safe Signer, a hardware signer for a Safe multisig. More every week.

Switch apps anytime at wedgie.dev/connect. Game saves stay when you switch or update.

Is it safe?

  • Plugging it in gives a computer nothing. It's locked.
  • Nothing installs or signs without a press of green on the wedgie itself. No website or program can press it.
  • Apps on the shelf and the firmware are signed by wedgie.dev. The wedgie checks before it installs.
  • Keys are made inside the secure chip and never leave it.

An app can do anything on the wedgie. Only install apps from the shelf, or code you trust.

Safe Signer

Makes your wedgie an owner of a Safe multisig. Install it, then go to wedgie.dev/safe.

  • The first time it opens, it says No key. Press green: the chip makes a key and locks it for good. Nobody ever sees it, not even us.
  • It shows what a transaction does. Green signs, red says no.
  • Keep a second owner on your Safe. If the chip breaks, its key is gone.

Make your own key. The first time you open Safe Signer it must say No key. If it already shows an address, someone made a key before you. Don't use it. Email us.

Make your own app

Go to wedgie.dev/code. Write it yourself, or have your AI agent write it. Try it in the browser, then put it on your wedgie. Agents: read wedgie.dev/skill.md.

If something's off

The screen doesn't light up
Turn the cable around: swap which end goes in the wedgie. Some only work one way. Still dark? Try another cable.
It doesn't show up on the computer
Try another cable (charge-only cables are common) and another USB port.
"No software"
Pick an app at wedgie.dev/connect.
An app is stuck or crashes when it starts
Hold X (the gray one next to red) while you plug it in. It starts without the app. Then pick another app.
"wedgie broke"
Press B to try again.
The page says the firmware is old
Click update and press green on the wedgie.
Still broken
Go to wedgie.dev/test in Chrome. It wipes the wedgie, puts everything back on, and tests the screen, buttons and chip. Your game saves come back. It never touches the chip's keys. To wipe it, push a paperclip into the small hole in the base while you plug it in.
